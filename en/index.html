The impact

What happens when the patient understands?

Understanding your diagnosis is a therapeutic act in itself.

🌿

Reduces anxiety

Patients with a better understanding of their diagnosis report less emotional distress during treatment.

Empowerment programs — MDPI Cancers 2024

💊

Improves adherence

Personalized educational interventions raise adherence to oral oncology medication from 87% to 97%.

PubMed 2015, Systematic Review

🏥

Fewer hospitalizations

Patients with low understanding have more ER visits and unplanned hospitalizations.

NCBI PMC7743046 — Lung Cancer Literacy

🤝

Shared decision-making

Informed patients ask their oncologist more questions and take an active role in their treatment plan.

Annals Cancer Epidemiology 2021

📋

Understands their own diagnosis better

A pathology report explained in accessible language raises patients' accuracy of knowledge about their cancer type and surgical margins to over 75%.

Randomized clinical trial, Support Care Cancer 2025

76% of studies on oncology education report improved clinical outcomes · Systematic Review LMICs, NCBI 2023

Purpose

Our mission and vision

🎯 Mission

To translate the pathology report, biomarkers, and precision medicine into language patients can understand: providing personalized support to those who need to decipher their own case, and educating collectively alongside patient associations, civil society organizations, and private companies —talks, workshops, forums, and a library of guides— so more people understand precision medicine in cancer, whether they need it yet or not. We strengthen the relationship with the treating team; we don't replace it.

💛 With love and patience 🔬 With scientific rigor 🤝 Patient-centered

⭐ Vision

To be, across different countries in Latin America, the reference in precision oncology education —the bridge of trust between science and the patient that patients, families, associations, civil society organizations, and private companies turn to when understanding the diagnosis and biomarkers defines how well treatment goes.

🌎 Partnerships across countries 🧬 Accessible biomarkers 🏅 Scientific validation

✅ What I do

I translate your pathology report and your biomarkers into language you can understand and bring with you to your appointment.

❌ What I don't do

I don't diagnose, prescribe treatment, give second opinions, or replace your oncology team. That work remains — and must remain — with your treating physician.

Our services

Three pillars that build patient empowerment

Each service translates pathology, biomarkers, and precision medicine into knowledge that's clear, warm, and scientifically grounded —for patients and families, and the associations that support them.

🩺
Service 01

Personalized Educational Consultation

A dedicated, unhurried space for the patient and their family to understand their pathology report and biomarkers.

  • I don't give clinical opinions or second opinions: I translate what your treating team already told you, I don't replace them
  • Pathology report and biomarkers explained step by step, in your own words
  • A dedicated space for patient and family, no rush
  • Biomarkers: HER2, PD-L1, KRAS, EGFR, BRCA, NGS, and more
  • Clear, empathetic language, free of jargon
🤝
Service 02

Strategic Partnerships

We join forces with patient associations to bring precision medicine and biomarker education to more people.

  • Talks, workshops, and forums on biomarkers and precision medicine, in simple language
  • Speaking engagements at patient association congresses and gatherings
  • Co-creation of scientifically validated educational content
  • One shared goal: adding knowledge toward patient empowerment
  • Content backed by science, free of commercial bias
📚
Service 03

Resource Library

Downloadable guides, in simple language, for each patient and their family to consult calmly, as many times as they need.

  • Guides in simple language on biomarkers: HER2, PD-L1, KRAS, NGS, and more
  • To consult calmly, at your own pace
  • Content reviewed and scientifically backed
  • First guides coming soon
